The transition from the Vessel to particular neurons contains the recording’s most substantial unresolved tension. I see at least two concerns operating there. One is the continuation of intelligence as an organized activity. Another is the continuation of this particular experiencing subject. Those concerns can overlap, but the monologue does not demonstrate that they are interchangeable.

A successor might preserve a person’s characteristic behavior, memories, vocabulary, or projects. Whether that would also preserve the same subject’s continuing experience is a further question. The suggestion that all material might eventually be replaced without interrupting continuity sits beside the fear that replacing circuit components might destroy identity.
